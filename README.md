# Entertainment-Haven
This is a movie-and-music themed landing page where you can browse film picks, listen to curated tracks, and explore a mood-based entertainment experience.

It combines a watch shelf and a listening queue in one unified experience for movie nights and relaxed listening sessions.
This website was created to help user find their moods and reduce time on searching for a perfect website 
Mood System

Most streaming sites make you search by genre. Entertainment Haven also lets you pick how you want to *feel*. Each mood groups movie genres and music genres that fit that feeling, so the site can match a film and a soundtrack together.

| Mood | Feeling | Movie genres | Music genres |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Comfort** | Warm, relaxed, low-stakes | Feel-good comedy, slice-of-life, family, food and cooking films, light romance | Lo-fi, acoustic, soft indie pop, easy jazz, bossa nova |
| **Chaos** | Fast, tense, high-energy | Action, thriller, heist/crime, dark comedy, adventure | Rock, electronic, hip-hop, punk, upbeat pop, big-band swing |
| **Heartbreak** | Emotional, bittersweet, reflective | Drama, romance, tragedy, coming-of-age | R&B, ballads, sad indie, singer-songwriter, piano pieces |
| **Wonder** | Dreamy, imaginative, awe-inspiring | Fantasy, sci-fi, animation, magical realism | Film scores, classical, ambient, dream pop, synth-wave |
| **Mystery** | Curious, uneasy, slow-burning | Mystery, noir, detective, psychological thriller | Dark ambient, trip-hop, minimal piano, moody synth, jazz noir |
| **Intense** | Frightening, tense, full of dread | Horror, supernatural, slasher, survival thriller | Dark ambient, eerie synth, dissonant strings, drone, haunted piano |

How the moods work

1. The user picks a mood (or clicks a movie).
2. The site filters the music queue to tracks tagged with the same mood.
3. The user can watch and listen at the same time, with the picture and the sound matched.

# Why moods instead of genres alone?

A genre describes *what* a title is. A mood describes *how it feels*. Two films from different genres, such as an animated fantasy and a sci-fi drama, can both feel like "Wonder," so they belong together with the same kind of music. Moods also work across movies and music, which genres don't do well on their own.

 Notes

- Some genres can fit more than one mood. A romantic comedy can be Comfort or Heartbreak depending on its story, so each title is tagged by how it feels, not only by its genre label.
- Mystery and Horror are separate on purpose. Mystery is about a puzzle ("what's going on?") and Horror is about fear ("get out of there!").
- Horror titles would carry an age rating or "mature" label in a full version.
